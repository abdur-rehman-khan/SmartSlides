Help & Instructions

🖐 Hand Gestures

  • Swipe right → — Next slide
  • Swipe left ← — Previous slide
  • Hold open palm ✋ — Toggle fullscreen (hold 0.4s)
  • Two hands spread apart 🤲 — Zoom in
  • Two hands close together — Zoom out
  • Good lighting and a plain background improve detection.
  • Keep your hand clearly visible in the camera frame.

🎤 Voice Commands — say ONE word

  • next — next slide
  • back — previous slide
  • first — jump to slide 1
  • last — jump to last slide
  • read — read slide text aloud
  • stop — stop reading
  • pause / resume — TTS control
  • repeat — read slide again
  • summarize — AI summary
  • explain — AI explanation
  • present — fullscreen mode
  • exit — close fullscreen

📁 Uploading

  • Only PowerPoint (.pptx) files are accepted.
  • Drag and drop, or click Browse.
  • Slides are rendered client-side — nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Text content is extracted for TTS and AI features.

✦ AI Features

  • Optional — requires a free Hugging Face token.
  • Get one at huggingface.co/settings/tokens
  • Paste it in the AI panel and click Save.
  • Works on slides with text content.
  • Token is only stored in your browser session.
